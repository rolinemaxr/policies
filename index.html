Privacy Policy

App: Calculator
Developer: Roline Tlumang
Last updated: June 1, 2026

Information We Collect

Calculator does not collect, store, or share any personal information. All calculations and measurements are performed locally on your device.

Device Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:

  • Device Orientation/Motion Sensor — used for the Spirit Level and Compass features only. This data is never stored or transmitted.

Data Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Calculation history
  • Saved measurements
  • Ruler calibration settings

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer.

Third Party Services

Calculator does not use any third party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
